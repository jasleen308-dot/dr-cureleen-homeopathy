Meet Dr. Jasleen

Curiosity Led Me Here

One of the important truths about me is that I was not always convinced about homeopathy. I have always believed in understanding something deeply before accepting it. During my medical education, my curiosity gradually drew me towards homeopathy through observation, study and understanding of its principles.

The philosophy of gentle, individualized and patient-centred care continues to guide my practice.

My Philosophy

I believe medicine should reduce suffering and help patients understand their health. Every patient is unique. Treatment should consider the individual, lifestyle, medical history and overall well-being.

My Promise To Patients

  • I will listen before I prescribe.
  • I will explain before I advise.
  • I will be honest about expectations.
  • Your health will always come before my ego.
